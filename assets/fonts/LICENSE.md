# Montserrat Font License

`Montserrat-ExtraBold.ttf` is a static instance of the Montserrat variable
font, licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

The full license text ships alongside the font in `OFL.txt`, as the licence
requires of any redistribution.

Upstream: https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat

## Hook fonts

`Anton-Regular.ttf` (https://github.com/googlefonts/AntonFont) and
`Bangers-Regular.ttf` (https://github.com/googlefonts/bangers) are also under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`), taken from
https://github.com/google/fonts. They draw the `slam` and `comic` hook styles.

Emoji are not vendored: `tools/hooks.py` fetches Noto Emoji PNGs (Apache 2.0,
https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji) into the cache on first use.
